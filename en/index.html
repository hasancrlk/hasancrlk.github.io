About

My focus is cybersecurity and digital forensics: network security, threat analysis, mobile device investigation, forensic imaging, RAM analysis and data recovery. I combine a security-first mindset with software development and build end-to-end solutions on the web and mobile side.

What I enjoy most in my projects is writing down why a rule is the way it is. "Why isn't a fixed window enough?", "why asyncio and not threading?", "is this check enforced in the UI or on the server?" The answers to these questions matter to me as much as the code itself.
